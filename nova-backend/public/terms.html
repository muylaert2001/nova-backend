Program Description

AVA is a personal AI assistant program that sends SMS notifications, reminders, and status updates to opted-in users.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on user-requested reminders and notifications. Typically a few messages per week.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opt-In and Opt-Out

Users opt in by providing their phone number directly to the account owner. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.
